About Me

BACKGROUND

I'm Li Yuchao, known as BG_LIT. An independent musician, keyboard player, and composer based in HK, currently perfecting my craft at HKDI. My journey began in secondary school, driven by a heartbeat of R&B and Hip-hop.

CURRENT PROJECT

Currently, I am deep in my graduation project—a complete manifestation of my identity. I handle everything: from the first lyric to the final mix. It's about breaking free and staying true.

FUTURE PLANS

Looking ahead, 2026 marks the arrival of my debut EP. I’m stepping out from the studio to the stage, sharing moments of raw emotion with everyone who listens.
